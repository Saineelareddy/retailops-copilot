# Punjab Sports Mart: Circular - Festive Season Return Window Extension
**Document ID:** CIRC-2026-05  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-10-01 to 2026-11-15  
**Authority:** Regional Head Office  

## Section 1: Seasonal Extension
1. For purchases made between October 1 and November 15, the standard return window for Sports Footwear is extended from 14 calendar days to **21 calendar days**.
2. All other provisions of POL-RET-2026 (including condition checks and Manager sign-off for refunds > ₹2,000) remain in full effect.
