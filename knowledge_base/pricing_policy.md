# Punjab Sports Mart: Store Pricing & Tag Discrepancy Policy
**Document ID:** POL-PRC-2026  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Authority:** Commercial Operations  

## Section 1: Customer Protection Price-Match Rule
If an item displays a lower price on the shelf label, tag, or display stand compared to the price retrieved by the barcode scanner / POS register:
1. The store **MUST honor the lower price** displayed on the shelf for the customer.
2. The cashier must manually apply a "Price Tag Discrepancy Override" on the POS register.

## Section 2: Shelf Correction Procedure
1. The cashier must immediately notify the floor supervisor.
2. The floor supervisor must inspect the shelf within 15 minutes and replace outdated price tags.
3. If systemic across multiple SKUs, a P4 support case must be logged.
