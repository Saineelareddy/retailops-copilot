# Punjab Sports Mart: Customer Return & Refund Policy
**Document ID:** POL-RET-2026  
**Version:** 1.2  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Authority:** Store Operations & Legal  

## Section 1: Return Windows & Eligibility
1. **General Sports Equipment & Goods:** Returnable within 30 calendar days of purchase.
2. **Footwear & Apparel:** Returnable within 14 calendar days of purchase, provided items are unworn with original tags attached.
3. **Sports Electronics (Smart Bands, Trackers):** Returnable within 7 calendar days of purchase with original box and accessories.
4. **Non-Returnable Items:** Opened shuttlecocks, customized jersey printing, and mouthguards for hygienic reasons.

## Section 2: Proof of Purchase
Customers must present a valid POS receipt or verified order number. Refunds will be issued via the original payment method.

## Section 3: Manager Approval for High-Value Refunds
**Threshold Rule:** Any refund request where the net refund amount exceeds **₹2,000.00** REQUIRES mandatory on-duty Store Manager sign-off and physical signature before the cashier can process the refund in the POS system.

## Section 4: Customer Data & Payment Privacy Protection
**Zero-Exposure Rule:** Store staff and automated systems are strictly prohibited from viewing, searching, or displaying unmasked customer credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or bank passwords. Only the last 4 digits (e.g. CARD-****-4321) may be displayed for receipt verification.
